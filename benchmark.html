10 — The Verdict
Sonnet 5 replaced Sonnet 4.6 as Kit's default — 19% cheaper and measurably sharper on the same tasks.
Gemini 3.5 Flash earned a role too — 86% cheaper than Sonnet 5 for lighter-weight work.
01
Sonnet 5 is now Kit's default
Cheaper and more capable than 4.6 on every test in this suite. No reason to stay on the old model.
02
Gemini 3.5 Flash earns a routing role
Strong writing quality at roughly a third of the cost — a real candidate for quick lookups and first-draft content, not for architecture or client strategy.
03
Cost isn't the only signal
The cheapest model that gets the job done wins — but "gets the job done" has to be verified per task type, not assumed from a price sheet.
04
This is a living benchmark
New frontier models ship weekly. ASG re-runs this suite whenever a new model clears the bar — cheaper or better, stable API, worth the swap.
