Verdict: Keep Haiku as portal default. Do not replace wholesale.

Haiku responds in 0.92s average on light portal tasks vs. Gemini 3.5 Flash's 2.42s — a 2.6x latency gap that matters for chat responsiveness. The cost difference is $0.00026 vs $0.00022 per call — roughly 4/100 of a cent, immaterial even at volume.

Gemini 3.5 Flash silently burns "thinking tokens" on every call, even trivial ones — part of why it's slower despite lower sticker pricing. It's a legitimate candidate for non-latency-sensitive background jobs (batch summarization, overnight processing) but not for anything the user is watching a spinner for.
